Positioning

Why not switch?

ePrashashan

Built for a single government school doing standard paperwork. Cannot run 6 trust units with hostel operations and parent portal in one system.

Entab / Fedena

Built for large private CBSE chains with 2,000+ students. Expensive, overkill for VSS, weak on trust hostel operations.

Teachmint

Good for online classes — not a full trust ERP. No hostel, no multi-unit, no fee depth.
