Need Why we built Aethon

The need

A custom platform for VSS and Dnyanda — designed around how we actually work.

The situation

  • One Samiti running schools and hostels together
  • 6 units — not one building, not one process
  • Paper registers, manual fees, delayed parent communication
  • No off-the-shelf software fit our model

What we needed

  • Unified system for hostel and schools
  • Attendance and biometric
  • Fee collection
  • Document generation and uploading
  • Timely message system
  • Self-healing software and website
  • And more — as the Samiti grew

Need What Aethon is

Not a product we bought. Ours.

Aethon is custom-developed for VSS Warora and Dnyanda. It runs on our servers. Our data stays with us. We are not ePrashashan — and we are not trying to be.

6 units, one system

School, junior college, and four hostels under one platform.

Our workflows

Fees, hostel, parents, staff — built how we operate.

We own it

No vendor license. The platform grows when we need it to.
